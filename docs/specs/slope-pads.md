# Slope pads

**Status:** idea
**Size classes:** all

## Purpose
Practise slope landings at increasing angles.

## Location
Foothills east and south-east of the field. The terrain survey found slopes of about 5° at 0.8 nm, 10° at 1.6 nm, 15° at 3.7 nm and 20° at 3.1 nm from the planned centre. Trees not checked.

## Layout
- A pad per slope angle, labelled with its angle.

## Player flow
- F10 menu: smoke at a pad, and its angle.

## Tests
- Build: pads at measured points, labelled with their angles.
- In game: whether the pads are clear of trees.

## Open questions
- Which angles to offer.
