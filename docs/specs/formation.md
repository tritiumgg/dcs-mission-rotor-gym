# Formation

**Status:** idea
**Size classes:** all

## Purpose
Hold position on a lead helicopter.

## Location
Around the field.

## Layout
- An AI helicopter flying a slow loop, spawned on request.

## Player flow
- F10 menu: spawn or remove the lead.
- Report the pilot's average distance from the lead and how much it varied.

## Tests
- Script: distance sampling and statistics, per player. Run against the DCS stub.

## Open questions
