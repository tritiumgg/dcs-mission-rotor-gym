# Oil rig

**Status:** planned
**Size classes:** labelled with the largest class that fits the rig's pad

## Purpose
Land on a small pad offshore, with no ground cues. Also a sling-load delivery spot.

## Location
About 1–2 nm off the coast west of the field. The planned anchor is in `src/layout.lua` (`areas`, "oil rig"). The coast is about 8 km west of the airfield.

## Layout
- DCS oil-platform statics with a helipad.

## Player flow
- F10 menu: smoke on the rig, and rig information.
